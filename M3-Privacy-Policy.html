Owners Repository for M3 — Privacy Policy

Owners Repository LLC · Updated September 22, 2026

This policy covers Owners Repository for M3 (also labeled Owners Repository for BMW M3 inside the app), provided by Owners Repository LLC. Android package: com.ownersrepository.bmwm3. It describes version 1.1.6 with a 48-hour trial and optional one-time unlock.

Your records

Vehicles, mileage, maintenance, tires, track notes, photos and receipts are stored on your device. The app does not send those records to Owners Repository or include an advertising or analytics SDK. No Owners Repository account is required.

Trial and purchases

The app stores a trial timer and cached purchase-access status on the device. Google Play handles checkout, payment information and purchase history under Google’s Privacy Policy. The app uses Google Play Billing to retrieve product prices, check completed and pending purchases, acknowledge purchases and restore access. Purchase tokens are used to acknowledge purchases with Google Play. Owners Repository does not receive your card details. There is no subscription and no automatic charge when the trial ends.

Permissions and files

Location permission is optional and supports the GPS lap timer while the app is in use. Location-derived session data remains on the device. The Android file picker grants access to files you select for receipt attachments, imports and exports. Internet permission supports Google Play Billing; remote network requests from the record interface are blocked.

Retention and control

Saved records remain viewable and exportable after the trial ends. Export backups to a location you control. Backups may contain personal information and receipt images. You control any sharing of them. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes locally held app data; exported copies remain wherever you saved them. Reinstalling requires restoring backups and, if purchased, restoring access with the purchasing Google account.

Security

Records are stored in app-local storage and are protected by Android’s app isolation and your device security. Use a screen lock and protect any exported copies. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request. Avoid sending unnecessary personal information. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence; information may be retained when needed to resolve an issue or meet applicable obligations.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The revised policy will show a new update date.

Children and contact

This app is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed to children under 13. We do not sell personal information. Contact robert@owners-repository.com for privacy questions.
