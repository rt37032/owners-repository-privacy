Privacy Policy

Owners Repository for Corvette
Last updated: September 23, 2026

Owners Repository for Corvette is designed as an on-device vehicle record system. The app does not require an account, does not sell personal information, and does not send users' vehicle records to Owners Repository LLC.

Information you provide

The app allows you to enter and store information such as vehicle details, identification numbers, mileage, maintenance and tire records, track notes, fuel calculations, ownership information, and other notes. You may also choose to add photographs, scanned receipts, or other documents.

Camera, photos, and files

If you choose to photograph or attach a receipt, vehicle image, or document, the app may request access to your camera, photos, or selected files. Access is used only to perform the action you request. You control which images and files are added.

Location

If you choose to use a feature that relies on location, such as a GPS-assisted track function, the app may request device location permission. Location access is used only while providing the requested feature. Owners Repository LLC does not receive or sell your location data.

Storage and processing

Vehicle records and user-added content are stored locally on your device unless you intentionally export, share, copy, or back them up using a service you select. Fuel calculations and other app functions are processed on the device. Owners Repository LLC does not operate a user-account database for this app.

Sharing

The app does not sell or rent personal information. Information is shared only when you intentionally use a device feature to export or share it, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users, the public, or our legal rights.

Third-party services

If you export or back up information through a third-party service, that service's privacy policy applies to its handling of the information. Google Play may independently collect diagnostic, purchase, security, and device information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Data retention and deletion

Your locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Before clearing data or uninstalling, export anything you wish to keep. If you shared or backed up information through another service, you must manage or delete that copy through the applicable service.

Security

We use reasonable measures in the app's design to protect locally stored information. No storage method is completely secure, so you should protect your device with a passcode and keep its operating system current.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for vehicle owners and enthusiasts and is not directed to children under 13. Owners Repository LLC does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The revised policy will display a new “Last updated” date.
