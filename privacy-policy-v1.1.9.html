Owners Repository for Porsche GT3 — Privacy Policy

Owners Repository LLC · Updated September 28, 2026

This policy covers version 1.1.9 of the Porsche GT3 Android app, including its 48-hour trial and optional one-time Google Play unlock. The app does not require an Owners Repository account.

Your records and documents

Vehicle details, mileage, maintenance, tire records, ownership details, track notes, vehicle photos, and receipt documents are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app does not send those records or documents to Owners Repository. It contains no advertising SDK and no developer-operated analytics SDK.

Trial and purchases

The app stores a device-local trial timer and cached purchase-access status. Google Play handles checkout, payment information, purchase history, localized prices, pending transactions, acknowledgements, and restoration under Google’s policies. The app uses Google Play Billing to check whether the one-time unlock is owned. Owners Repository does not receive or store your card details. There is no subscription and no automatic charge when the trial ends.

Location

Location permission is optional and is requested only when you enable the GPS lap timer. If granted, the feature uses foreground location, including precise location when available, to set a start/finish line and calculate lap times. Saved GPS sessions may include start/finish coordinates, accuracy, timestamps, speed-derived information, and lap times. This information remains in the app’s private storage and is not sent to Owners Repository. The app does not request background location. You can deny or revoke location permission and continue using non-GPS features.

Selected files, sharing, imports, and exports

The Android file picker grants the app access only to files you select for vehicle photos, receipt attachments, backup import, or backup export. You may also share a supported receipt image, PDF, or text file to the app. Selected attachments are copied into app-private storage. Exported JSON backups contain your saved records and may include embedded copies of vehicle photos and receipt documents, so store and share backups carefully.

Internet access and Google Play

Internet access supports Google Play Billing. The app’s vehicle-record interface is restricted to packaged app content and app-private attachment files; it blocks remote web navigation and remote resource requests. Google Play and related Google components may process technical, device, account, and transaction information as described in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Saved records remain viewable and exportable after the trial ends. You may delete individual records or use “Clear all data” in Settings to remove repository records and app-private attachments while keeping trial and purchase-access status. Clearing the app’s storage through Android Settings or uninstalling removes all local app data, including trial state; exported backups remain wherever you saved them. A Google Play purchase can be restored with the purchasing Google account, but purchase restoration does not restore vehicle records.

Data sale, advertising, and children

Owners Repository does not sell personal information and does not use your records for advertising. This app is intended for vehicle owners and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app features, dependencies, or legal requirements change. The public policy is available at owners-repository.com/privacy.html. Contact support@owners-repository.com with privacy questions.
